How It Works

  1. Enter the From Address — the address with spendable UTXOs.
  2. Enter the To Address — recipient's Bitcoin address.
  3. Enter the amount in satoshis.
  4. The backend signs the transaction with ECDSA using the private key associated with your address.
  5. The signed transaction is verified against the P2PKH script.
  6. If valid, it is broadcast to the mempool and relayed to peers.
  7. Miners pick it up and include it in the next block.
